# Traditional Catholic App Market Assessment

## Positioning

The Ordinary Catholic should not compete as another general prayer app. Hallow owns polished guided prayer and habit formation. Laudate owns broad free utility. iPieta owns deep traditional reference. Sanctifica owns traditional calendar, routines, and daily devotional structure. Truthly owns Catholic AI chat.

The strongest lane is a beautiful, source-first traditional Catholic home base:

- Traditional doctrine without apology.
- Latin and English side by side.
- Douay-Rheims, Vulgate, and modern translation comparison.
- Old catechisms, Fathers, Aquinas, councils, papal archives, and New Advent references.
- Traditional Latin Mass formation.
- Reverent explanations of current questions: altar rails, receiving on the tongue, ad orientem, chant, veiling, fasting, and liturgical continuity.
- AI/chat only when every answer shows sources and authority tiering.

## Competitor Signals

- Hallow: guided prayer, routines, audio, challenges, habit streaks, polished onboarding.
- Laudate: large prayer utility, daily readings, Bible, Latin prayers, user prayer folders.
- iPieta: Douay-Rheims, Vulgate, Haydock, Fathers, Summa, Baltimore Catechism, Roman Catechism, TLM and Novus Ordo calendar, highlights, bookmarks.
- Sanctifica: traditional calendar, Latin Mass readings, Breviary, Martyrology, habits, routines, audio.
- Truthly: Catholic AI answers, daily learning, shareable answers.

## Product Gaps To Own

- Translation intelligence: not just parallel Bible text, but why differences matter.
- TLM formation: a guided school for first-timers and long-time trad Catholics.
- Reverence explainers: rails, tongue, kneeling, silence, chant, veils, sacred architecture.
- Source authority map: Vatican, councils, old catechisms, Fathers, Aquinas, New Advent, Catholic Answers.
- Traditional current topics: charitable, grounded, clear, never contemptuous toward lawful ordinary-form Catholics.
- Priest/bishop guest columns: curated commentary separated from doctrine and clearly labeled.
- Latin learning layer: Mass responses, prayer pronunciation, vocabulary, toggled translation.

## MVP Must Become

- Robust Trad Vault with many source shelves.
- Bible Compare with Douay-Rheims, Vulgate phrase, modern Catholic translation, and doctrinal notes.
- TLM School with Ordo, gestures, responses, and glossary.
- Prayer Treasury with Latin/English toggle and audio-ready structure.
- Ask with strict cited-source behavior.
- Current Questions center for traditional issues.

## Brand Tone

Serious, beautiful, reverent, masculine enough to avoid generic beige devotional softness, but not combative. The posture is: "Here is the faith handed down; here are the sources; come deeper."
